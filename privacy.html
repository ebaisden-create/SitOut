Privacy

When you join the SitOut waitlist we store your email address, the time you signed up, your place in line, and, only if you used the calculator, the monthly amount you entered.

We use your email only to tell you when SitOut launches and to give founding members their free access. We never sell or share it. To be removed, email us at CONTACT_EMAIL and we will delete it.

To stop spam, your browser solves a small math puzzle when you sign up (no tracking and no third-party captcha), and we keep a one-way scrambled version of your IP address for up to 24 hours to limit repeat attempts. We can't turn it back into your IP address.
